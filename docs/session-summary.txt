Rationale: recall failures are worse than precision failures — a record you never see you can never buy.


ML DECISIONS

Classifier architecture. Logistic regression chosen over neural model for Stage 2 because: interpretable, fast to train and retrain nightly, works well on sparse TF-IDF features, sufficient for 50k training examples. Neural bandit (Stage 3) handles the richer feature set after enrichment.

Bigrams used (ngram_range=(1,2), max_features=20000). Captures collector phrases like "blue note original", "first press stereo", "white label promo" as single features rather than co-occurring unigrams.

Stop words reconsidered. Words like promo, wlp, og, original carry real collector signal and were previously stripped. Removed from STOP_WORDS so TF-IDF can learn from them.

TF-IDF retrained nightly. POST to /ml/tfidf/rebuild/ refits vectorizer on full current keeper corpus (DiscogsRecord where evaluated=True and wanted=True), saves new model, marks old inactive. Nightly retrain covers bandit, TF-IDF, and first-pass classifier.

Bandit description (for portfolio/writeup). Neural contextual bandit with a contrastive encoder. The contrastive component learns a personalized embedding space by pulling together items accepted and pushing apart items rejected, capturing the latent structure of individual preferences rather than aggregate popularity signals. The bandit layer uses Thompson sampling to balance exploiting known preferences with exploring uncertain candidates, surfacing hidden value in items that don't look obviously desirable on the surface but structurally resemble what has historically been chosen.


PRICING

Max bid formula revised. Previous formula (sliding margin based on fee rate) set bids too low — $45 record got $18.71 max bid, not tenable. New formula:

    fraction = 0.75 - 0.273 * exp(-0.02 * sell_price)
    bid = sell_price * fraction - shipping

Calibrated to: $30 sell → $12 max bid, $45 → $22.76, $100 → $65.30, $200 → $143. Fraction asymptotes to 0.75 at high prices. Shipping assumed $6 flat.
